Licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later.
The full licence text is in licenses\SecureUxTheme-COPYING.txt.

The installer carries the official, unmodified SecureUxTheme MSI packages from the project's
GitHub release (the SHA-256 digests are checked at build time). Its complete source code for
that release is at:

    https://github.com/namazso/SecureUxTheme/tree/v4.0.0

SecureUxTheme is installed as its own product. It shows up as "SecureUxTheme" in
Settings > Apps > Installed apps and is uninstalled from there. Uninstalling archwindows leaves
it installed.

Visual styles (msstyles\)
-------------------------
The bundled .msstyles files are recoloured copies of the Windows Aero visual style
(aero.msstyles, (c) Microsoft Corporation), made by tools\make_msstyles.py.
